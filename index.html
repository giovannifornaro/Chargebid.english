The marketplace for EV charging infrastructure

The name

Charge — la ricarica: l'energia che incontra la mobilità. Ogni punto di ricarica è un nodo. Ogni nodo è un accordo che aspetta di essere fatto.  ·  Bid — dall'inglese bid (offerta, asta): il meccanismo competitivo che connette chi ha lo spazio con chi vuole operare. Non una trattativa bilaterale — un'asta strutturata dove più operatori competono per la stessa location. ChargeBid porta quella logica fondante: uno scambio infrastrutturale neutrale, many-to-many, nato in Europa, per l'Europa.

ChargeBid connects those who own space with those who need it — enabling CPOs to find, contract, trade and optimise charging locations across Europe at speed.

Established CPOs with contracted locations are open to letting and swapping assets. There is no structured market for this today. Every deal is a one-off phone call. ChargeBid institutionalises what already happens informally.

The institutional precedent

Wren House already bet on this model — in telecom

Wren House — one of Electrip Global's principal shareholders — invested in Phoenix Tower International (PTI): the world's largest private tower platform, with 33,000+ towers across 23 countries. PTI owns the towers and leases them to operators. The model — aggregating infrastructure, connecting operators to locations — is institutionally validated. ChargeBid is the asset-light, marketplace-pure version of that logic applied to EV: no assets on the balance sheet, pure matching. Lighter capital, higher margins, faster scale.

Regulatory tailwind

AFIR now mandates a charger every 60km — enforcement tightens

EU Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation already mandates 3M+ public chargers by 2030. In Settembre 2026, the European Parliament moved to sharpen the density rule: a mandatory charging point every 60 kilometres on the TEN-T core network. This transforms a volume target into a geographic mandate — creating urgent, location-specific demand that CPOs cannot fulfil without a structured site-sourcing tool. ChargeBid is that tool.

The core rule of the trading module

Reciprocity is mandatory

Every CPO that acquires a location through trading must also list at least one asset. This is not optional — it is embedded in the platform rules. It ensures liquidity flows in both directions and prevents the market from becoming one-sided.

⇄
If you buy, you must offer.
Reciprocity is contractual. No reciprocal listing = no trading access. This creates a self-reinforcing market.

Model di Ricavo

Six revenue streams. All anchored to real data.

One recurring anchor. Four transactional. One data. Every number derives from explicit, verifiable assumptions.

Stream A

CPO Subscription

€10K Anno1 · €20K Anno2 · €25–40K Anno3+

Annual fee to access the full database, geo-intelligence, bidding tools and trading module. Entry ticket to every other stream. Recurring, non-dilutable.

Formula: Subscribed CPOs × fee media ponderata per coorte. Nuovi CPO contribuiscono al 50% nell'anno di ingresso. Fee media cresce da €5K (2027) a €20,7K (2031).
Stream B

Closing commission

€600 – €4,000 per deal · avg €1,400

Retained on every agreement closed on the platform. One-time at signing, via clearing house. Any landlord bonus (max €10K on sites with capex above €250K) is separate from the ChargeBid commission.

Formula: deals/year × weighted avg commission €1,400.
Mix: 60% small (€600, capex <€30K) · 30% medium (€2,000, capex €30–250K) · 10% large (€4,000, capex >€250K).
Stream C

Revenue share — 1% annual

€0 (2027) → €556K (2031) → €22,762K (2035)

1% on charging revenues generated by active locations. Only 20% of published locations are successfully bid. Revenue share starts 12 months after the bid — no revenue in Y1. The base grows by accumulation: each year adds the deals closed in the previous year.

Formula: cumulative deals closed in prior years × 1.5 chargers × kWh/day × €0.25 × 365 × 1%.
Consumption starts at 50 kWh/day/charger, +35% YbY. Closed deals = 20% of published locations.
Y1: €0 · Y2: 100 loc × 1.5 × 67.5kWh × €0.25 × 365 × 1% = €9K.
Stream D

Letting — commission on charger rental

€50K (2027) → €253K (2031)

Il letting è un affitto vero: un CPO o OEM affitta la stazione di un altro operatore. ChargeBid percepisce il 5% annuale sul canone di affitto. Canone di riferimento: €10.000/anno per charger. Riservato ai Subscribed CPOs.

Formula: chargers in letting × €10,000/year × 5% commission = €500/charger/year.
Y1: 100 chargers × €500 = €50K. Growth +50% YbY.
Stream E

NPS & Rating Analytics

€20K (2027) → €320K (2031)

Premium data product: landlord NPS scores, CPO reliability ratings, location performance benchmarks. Sold to CPOs and property managers. The proprietary dataset ChargeBid accumulates — impossible to replicate without the platform's transaction history.

Formula: data product subscribers × annual fee €10K.
Y1: 2 clients. Grows with dataset maturity.
Stream F

Listing fee — location publication

€0 (2027) → €338K (2031)

From Y2 landlords pay to publish their location. Basic listing €50 · premium listing €100. Free in Y1 to accelerate supply growth. From Y2 applies to 30% of platform locations.

Formula: total locations × 30% × avg fee €75 (50/50 basic/premium mix).
Y2: 1,200 × 30% × €75 = €27K. Scales with platform locations.

Platform Expansion

One engine. Three infrastructure verticals.

The ChargeBid matching engine — location owners on one side, operators on the other — is not specific to EV charging. It is a generic infrastructure marketplace architecture. Once the platform is live and the technology is validated, the same engine can be replicated across three adjacent verticals without rebuilding from zero.

Vertical 02 — Telco

Tower tenant acquisition

Tower companies (including PTI, backed by Wren House) own 33,000+ sites globally and must find tenants — mobile operators, broadcasters, ISPs — for each one. Today this is a slow, bilateral, manual process. ChargeBid Telco replicates the EV marketplace logic: landowners list tower sites, operators bid for access. Same subscription + commission model.

Strategic link: Wren House (Electrip shareholder) backs PTI. ChargeBid Telco is a natural tool for PTI's tenant acquisition at scale — and a ready-made acquisition or licensing target for the Wren House portfolio.
Vertical 03 — Solar

Solar farm site matching

Solar energy developers need large land parcels with grid connection access, specific orientation, and planning permission. Landowners — particularly agricultural — have underutilised land generating zero yield. ChargeBid Solar connects the two: landowners list eligible sites, solar operators bid for 20–30 year lease agreements. Same two-sided dynamic, high-value, long-duration contracts.

Market size: Europe needs 600GW of solar by 2030 (REPowerEU). Each GW requires ~8–10km² of land. The site-matching problem is structural and massive.
Vertical 04 — Data Centres

Server farm & edge compute location

Edge data centre and server farm operators need sites with power density, cooling capacity, fibre access and planning clearance. Industrial landowners — warehouses, brownfield, logistics parks — hold compatible assets they cannot easily monetise. ChargeBid Data Centre matches operator demand with industrial supply. High-value, long-term, B2B-only deals.

Convergence: PTI already builds edge data centres at the base of its towers. ChargeBid Telco and ChargeBid Data Centre share the same industrial landowner supply base — one platform, compounding network effects.

What ChargeBid does differently
Lato domanda B2B — solo CPO. I landlord listano gratuitamente; solo i Subscribed CPOs fanno offerte. This flips the model: ChargeBid monetises the operator (demand), not the landlord (supply). Higher LTV, better unit economics.
Primary + secondary market. ChargeBid originates new infrastructure deals (undeveloped sites → CPO deployment) and enables CPO-to-CPO trading of existing assets. SiteBid only touches the secondary.
Multi-vertical by design. EV now, Telco and Solar next. The same landlord who lists a car park for EV may list adjacent land for a solar lease. One platform, compounding supply.
Deep geo-intelligence. Predictive location scoring (traffic, grid access, planning risk, competitor density), CPO reliability ratings, NPS scores — a proprietary data layer that compounds with every transaction and cannot be replicated without the transaction history.
The validation signal

SiteBid proves that operators and landowners will transact on a neutral platform. It has attracted institutional advisors and real deal flow in the most mature infrastructure market in the world. ChargeBid is not copying SiteBid — it is building the version that never existed: B2B-first, multi-sector, data-enriched, and purpose-built for Europe's regulatory and infrastructure context.
